This is what's new in this week's update:

New quests for Level 12 players:
If you're level 12 your horseshoes are probably pretty worn out by now. Ride to the blacksmith Conrad Marsdeen in Moorland and see if he's got any ideas.

New features:

  • Last week we could see construction sites here and there around Jorvik and there are hotels and hostels that have been built.
When you want it to be a new day, for example, to continue a quest you can just check in to any of the hotels or hostels and sleep over! When you wake up it's a new day and you can continue with the quest!

  • For easier access to Valedale Lake and Mr. Andersson there is now a horse trailer at the lake.
