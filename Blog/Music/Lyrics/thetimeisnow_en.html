Lisa has been busy and released another single! The Time Is Now is about sisterhood and love that can conquer anything. Together we are stronger and it takes courage to face evil, but together we can do it.

The Time is Now - to stand together

With everything going on in the world, listening to The Time Is Now can bring a little hope. Hope to love each other and face troubles together, united.
