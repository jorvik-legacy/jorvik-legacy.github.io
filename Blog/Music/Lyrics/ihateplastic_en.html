I HATE PLASTIC!

DJ Kai is the main party starter on Jorvik! Every now and then, she uses her platform as an artist to make a statement. With the release of her single, I Hate Plastic, her statement comes across loud and clear!

The people of Jorvik have a complicated relationship with the plastics industry. Plastic is made by processing crude oil extracted from the Earth, draining non-renewable resources and putting Jorvik’s wildlife at risk. At the same time, Dark Core’s offshore oil rig provides jobs to many of the island’s residents.
