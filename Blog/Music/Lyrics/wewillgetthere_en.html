WE WILL GET THERE!

We Will Get There is the new single from our very own Lisa Peterson! It’s a song about hope and strength – that no matter what life throws at you, We Will Get There. Lisa’s latest was recorded in L.A. with awesome producer Garrison Barr. Sing along with the lyrics for We Will Get There and play along with the chords sheet music!
