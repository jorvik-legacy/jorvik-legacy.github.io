2011. november 16., szerda

Wednesday update!

Updates will continue to be released every Wednesday, ranging from new quests to new features.
Because of this, the server will be down between 10:00 a.m. and 2:00 p.m!

New Quests:

  • Tan has lost his money. Help him find it..
  • Donald (now Daxton) wants to give you a gift for always bringing him wool. He’s asking for your help with this.

New Feature:
Did you know that if you click on the player standing next to you, you can find out information about them? If you click on the player, it displays their name, level, and the name and level of their horse. If you click on the small portrait that appears, you can invite them to be your friend!

The daily tasks have changed. Some are only available on certain days, and that’s exactly why you’ll receive a larger amount of money and reputation points as a reward.

Issues related to race results have been fixed.
