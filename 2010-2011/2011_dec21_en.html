2011. december 21., szerda

Christmas continues!


New feature:

  • Riding Clubs: From now on, anyone can create a club—just speak to the Grand Master of the Riding Club in Moorland or Silverglade Village. When you create a club, you become its owner, so you decide who to invite and what titles to give them. Only you can remove players or close the club. You can only be a member of one riding club at a time. You cannot leave your club without closing it completely. To create your own club, you must be a Star Rider, at least Level 5, and it costs 50 star coins. A club can have up to 50 members and has its own chat room where only members can post and only they can see the messages. The club also has its own section where you can post the club rules and events.
  • In addition to the club chat, teams also have their own chat rooms.

Jumping is now tied to Star Rider membership. In other words, you can only jump if you are a Star Rider!

Competition results have been reset.

Player colors have been updated:

  • white – all regular players
  • light green – friends
  • light yellow – you’re in the same club
  • light blue – you’re on the same team
