2011. december 14., szerda

Christmas continues in Jorvik!


New Feature:

  • My Stable, or "My Home," is available exclusively to Star Rider members. Since inventory space is limited, you can store clothes and equipment you don't want to carry with you in the closet.
  • In addition, you can choose a stable for yourself. Initially, everyone belongs to the Moorland stable, but you can change this to one of the following: Fort Pinta and Steve’s Farm.

Additional changes:

  • A road icon appears next to the mini-map when you're on a road. This means you'll ride faster in those areas.
  • Mailbox: When you receive a new message, a small red flag appears next to the mini-map.
  • Minor fixes and updates.
