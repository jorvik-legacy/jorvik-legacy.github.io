Fallen stars!

Hello everyone!
Stars have fallen all over Jorvik and your mission is to collect them all!

Collect Stars:
Stars have fallen from the sky and landed all over Jorvik. Try to find as many as you can! If you open the map and click on the discovered areas tab you'll be able to see in what areas you can find the stars and how many you have collected. Click on the star when you find it to collect it!

New Western styled Bridles:
Western styled Bridles have arrived to Jorvik! You will be able to find them in Fort Pinta, Moorland Stables, Valedale, Silverglade Village and Cape West Fishing Village!

Chat Changes:
After many requests and conversations with our players we are making significant changes to the chat in Star Stable. The "Stable Chat" has now been replaced with a channel called "Global Chat". Unlike the Stable Chat which was confusing and could only be used in Stable areas, the Global Chat can now be used from anywhere in Star Stable. We feel this will make it easier for everyone to stay in touch and to ask for help where and when you need it!

In the interests of keeping the Global Chat a friendly and spam-free environment only paying Star Riders, Lifetime members or previously paying Star Riders will be able to use the Global Channel. Free players will still be able to read the chat, but will not be able to participate in the Global Chat. We want the Global Chat to remain a great place to connect with your friends in Star Stable! Allowing unlimited access to the Global Chat feature to everyone would unfortunately make this impossible.
