2015. október 28., szerda

SCARIEST. HALLOWEEN. EVER.

So now it’s time to turn Star Stable into SCARE STABLE! It’s time for Jorvik’s spookiest Halloween ever!

Spooktacular New Halloween Quest!
Rob and Bob have been out to sea, and they’ve brought back something that isn’t everything it seems, and is honestly more than just a little bit scary… Head over to Golden Hills Valley to see them and find out what’s going on, but be careful! It’s not safe anymore in the Goldenleaf Forest. At least not when she is there.

Warning for players:
This quest is lots of fun and can be pretty nerve-racking at times, but could possibly be genuinely scary for some of our younger players. If scary stories make you nervous, then it’s fine to ask someone at home to hang out with you while you play through this quest chain! Remember, it’s all just a story :)

To get started with this quest, you need to be a Star Rider and have completed the quest where you help Bob out of the water after he’s fallen in.

Terrifyingly Fun Halloween Clothes!
Now’s the chance to get totally dressed up for Halloween! A whole bunch of bloodcurdlingly awesome new styles are available at the Mall, and don’t forget to check out all the new pop-up Halloween stores all over Jorvik! They’ve got some spine-chilling surprises in store.

Hair-Raising Amazing Makeup!
To match your Halloween outfit, why not check out the salons, where you’ll find some monstrous makeup to give yourself that supernaturally sensational Halloween look!

Ghoulishly Fun Apple-Bobbing
All over Jorvik, the islanders are getting ready for frighteningly fun bobbing for apples! In a range of different locations, you’ll get the chance to play the traditional Halloween game of grabbing an apple with your teeth from a barrel full of water, and guess what? There’s prizes! It’s our ghostly guarantee that these prizes are scarily awesome! Either something edible for your horse, or some cool all-new horse gear!

The special prizes will be in a different location each day, so get exploring and find those Halloween giveaways!

Gruesome, Ghostly Goings-on!
Sadly, Ghostbusters don’t have an office on Jorvik so it’s up to you to track down the startling spooks that have taken over the island. You’ll find these frightful phantoms in all kinds of hidden corners of Jorvik, and you’ll only be able to work out where they are by listening carefully for their spooky noises!

Once you hear them, you’ll have to try and find them! They stay invisible until you get close enough, then BOO! They’re right in front of you. Once you’ve found the little creepers, you’ll bag yourself a reward which could be something edible for your horse, or something cute to wear for yourself! This year’s clothing and horse gear rewards in the Halloween quests are all brand new!

To get started on this ghostly task, talk to Mrs Holdsworth - she'll tell you what to do!

Horse Market
Eddie and Ferdinand are getting the Horse Market ready for another week of trading, so they’re closed right now. Eddie tells us that she chose this week on purpose so that the horses wouldn’t get too spooked.

Have tons and tons of spooky fun, StarFam!
Hugs from the Star Stable team x
